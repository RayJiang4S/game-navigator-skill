# Evidence and exploration protocol

## Evidence order by question

- Current location, equipment, resources, quest state: current API/log/save/UI observation.
- Skill, item, or equipment comparison: current ownership/equipped state joined to a build-valid entity definition; do not substitute an icon or name for target, hits, cost, potency, or restrictions.
- Current rules and controls: build-matched in-game text, official manual, official patch notes.
- Hidden formula, drop rate, or interaction: reproducible local experiment or independently auditable primary analysis.
- Build, route, purchase, or equipment advice: current player state plus the player's goal and several viable strategies.
- Story consequence or missable content: build-matched evidence and the user's explicit spoiler allowance.

Do not turn repeated search results into independent confirmation. Assign the same `independenceGroup` to mirrors, rewrites, videos quoting the same test, and pages derived from one wiki or post. Record parent/reprint/revision relationships when known.

## Before giving significant advice

1. Confirm AppID, BuildID, platform, DLC, mode, difficulty, and current state where relevant.
2. Split a page into atomic testable claims. Never store an entire guide as one claim.
3. Link actual supporting, refuting, or qualifying evidence.
4. Mark conflicts instead of averaging them away.
5. Raise the evidence threshold for permanent skill points, unique resources, missable story outcomes, purchases, and save-changing actions.
6. Prefer a reversible next action if the evidence remains incomplete.

## New or undocumented games

Treat missing guides as an exploration opportunity:

1. Capture a baseline and identify what cannot yet be observed.
2. State competing hypotheses and a result that would distinguish them.
3. Design one-variable, reversible, low-cost steps with explicit stop conditions.
4. Capture before and after state; include the player's feel and execution difficulty.
5. Complete the experiment as supported, refuted, inconclusive, or aborted.
6. Promote a result only within the tested Build and conditions. Retest after an update.

Never consume a unique item, cross a known irreversible story boundary, damage a save, bypass anti-cheat, or expose hidden competitive information merely to test a hypothesis.

## Answer contract

For an important decision, answer in this order:

1. What the live state confirms.
2. What a build-matched rule or evidence confirms.
3. What remains uncertain or conflicts.
4. The safest useful next step and why it fits the player's stated goal.
5. An optional low-risk verification.
6. Stronger spoilers or irreversible consequences only when requested.
